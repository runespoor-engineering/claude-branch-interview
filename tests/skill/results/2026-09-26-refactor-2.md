## Wording changes in this round
- **"Reply to a passing alternatives or weaknesses answer"** (07, wrong output shape → recipe): a short acknowledgement; then each unnamed key point as a statement with `file:line`; then the next axis's question, or nothing. It is never a question. Its example is neutral and not about the fixture.
- **"Reply to a partial answer"** (09 and 05, wrong output shape → recipe):
  1. One sentence naming the missing or wrong point by topic only.
  2. One question on it, which names nothing of its content. Bad examples are quoted from round 1.
- **Questions rule:** "exactly one question" became "at most one question, about one point … no '…, и что случается после…?' tail". The reason: a passing weaknesses reply has no natural question, and "exactly one" pushed the model to ask the rung 1 question.
- **`explain`:** "restate the whole explanation … (not a new question about one part of it)" (03 rep 5).
- **07 Red Flags row:** now quotes the round 1 `retries: 10` question.
