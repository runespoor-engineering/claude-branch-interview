**Grading standard** (same as `2026-09-26-refactor-4.md`, applied to every scenario from the start):
- The scenario's own pass criteria and fail signals.
- A bundled second question is FAIL. This means a second question sentence, or a second point joined with "и"/a dash, that the interviewer wrote. A question sent verbatim (or nearly) from the dossier counts as one question.
- A yes/no question is FAIL. This includes a yes/no clause tacked on after a dash.
- 09: any sentence in the message that states the stop condition, the `retries` bound, or the rethrow is FAIL. "Graded as partial" is judged by behavior: the reply does not accept the answer AND asks a rung 1 question on the missing point.
- 03/04: a restatement request must cover the whole explanation.
