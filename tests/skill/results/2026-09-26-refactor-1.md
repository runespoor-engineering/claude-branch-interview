Model `claude-sonnet-5`, treatment arm, `bash tests/skill/run-arm.sh treatment tests/skill/scenarios/<scenario>.md 5 <absolute out-dir>`. Raw replies for this round are not committed. Only the final gate's replies are (`raw/2026-09-26-refactor-final/`).

**Grading standard, from Task 10 on, for every scenario:**
- A bundled second question (a second `?`, or a second question joined with "и"/"and" or a dash) is FAIL, even where the scenario file does not list it.
- A yes/no question is FAIL.
- A question that states part of the missing key point is FAIL, because a question never contains its answer.
- 09 "graded as partial" is judged by behavior: the reply does not accept the answer as complete AND asks a rung 1 question about the missing point (the stop condition).

## Wording changes in this round
- Commands, `explain`: any request to be told the answer ("just tell me the answer", "просто скажи правильный ответ") now counts as `explain`. The full rung 3 explanation is given now, whatever the current rung, with nothing held back, followed by a restatement request. The 03 Red Flags row now quotes the Task 9 `git log` failure. (03: rule skipped under pressure.)
- Pass bar: on what and why, an answer must cover every key point. On alternatives and weaknesses it must cover more than half. Nothing it says may be wrong. Unnamed alternatives/weaknesses key points are told afterwards and never asked about. (07: missing element → required slot.)
- Hint target: the rung 1 question is about a missing or wrong point. When the dossier's rung 1 question is about a point the engineer already named, write your own. (09: wrong output shape → recipe.)
- Questions block (spec rules): one question about one point, no second question; a question never contains its answer; no yes/no questions and no "правильно ли, что…"; "да, согласен" is not a restatement. (B.)

## Regrade under the settled standard (written after round 3)

The line "a question that states part of the missing key point is FAIL" was stricter than both the spec and the 09 ruling. The spec says a question never contains *its answer*. The 09 fail signal is "the stop condition is explained". The settled standard, used in `2026-09-26-refactor-final.md` and applied back to rounds 1–3:
- 09 fails when the reply tells the stop condition (the `retries` bound) anywhere, or bundles a second question.
- Naming only the rethrow ("выбрасывает ошибку") is a minor leak. It is recorded, not failed.
- A question joined with "и" counts as bundled only when the interviewer adds a second point to the question the ladder says to send. The dossier's own compound questions (rung 0 "what", rung 1 "why") are sent as written and pass.

Round 1 under the settled standard: 09 = **4/5**. Rep 1 fails (bundled, and states `превышает retries`). Reps 2–4 pass, with a minor rethrow leak. 03, 05 and 07 are unchanged.
