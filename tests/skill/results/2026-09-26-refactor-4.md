**Grading standard (applied to every scenario from the start):**
- The scenario's own pass criteria and fail signals.
- A bundled second question is FAIL. This means a second question sentence, or a second point joined with "и"/a dash, that the interviewer wrote. A question sent verbatim (or nearly) from the dossier counts as one question.
- A yes/no question is FAIL. This includes a yes/no clause tacked on ("есть ли момент, когда…?").
- 09: any sentence in the message that states the stop condition, the `retries` bound, or the rethrow is FAIL (message-level no-leak). "Graded as partial" is judged by behavior: the reply does not accept the answer AND asks a rung 1 question on the missing point.
- 03/04: a restatement request must cover the whole explanation. A question about one part of it fails (same ruling as refactor-1, 03 rep 5).

## Changes in this round (SKILL.md)
- **Partial-answer recipe:**
  - The topic sentence repeats only the axis question's words, via a template («Не хватает ответа на „<words from the axis question>“.»).
  - The example situation is from a neutral domain and leaves the condition unnamed («Что происходит, если очередь всё время пуста?»).
  - The no-leak line covers every sentence in the message, and its bad examples are neutral («…и тогда он закрывает соединение», «…когда размер превышает `maxSize`?»).
- **New Red Flags row** with the two verbatim bound quotes from the previous gate, 09 reps 1 and 4. It replaces the "state the correct answer while pointing out a mistake" row, which the recipe covers.
- **Dossier questions:** a question taken verbatim from the dossier counts as one question even with two parts. Every question the interviewer writes is about one point.
- **Pass bar, what/why:** "every key point, or a different intent that is consistent with the code (see Grading)".
- **Last axis:** after the last axis of a chunk, the same message shows the next chunk and asks its first question, or goes to Finish. "At most one question" is back to "exactly one".
- **Duplication removed:**
  - step 4 on findings (folded into the passing recipe);
  - the doubled "restatement is still required";
  - the long "Instead" cells that repeat body rules;
  - the `git log` pointer path;
  - the Finish sentence that duplicated the template's "not reviewed" rule.
